## Background

This case study focuses on developing an AI-powered business-to-business (B2B) sales intelligence
platform specifically targeted at generating leads for the sales team at a roofing distributor.
The platform will leverage public data sources, particularly GAF, to pre-generate actionable sales
insights and recommendations for this sales team to review during account planning. The goal is to
create a comprehensive solution that helps sales teams identify, understand, and effectively engage
with decision-makers.

## Objectives

1. **Intuitive UI**
   - There must be a way for reps to view the leads the system generates.
   - Bonus points if the interface is visually polished, presents information in a clear and
     intuitive way, or includes distinctive or creative features.
2. **Robust data management**
   - Your solution will likely need a way to store, organize, and retrieve data. We are looking
     for approaches that would be suitable for a production environment.
   - Due to time constraints, it is acceptable to outline future improvements or describe how your
     approach could evolve in a full production system.
3. **Scalable pipeline**
   - Whatever pipeline you implement should be designed with scale in mind, as it may eventually
     support hundreds or thousands of sales representatives. Consider how your approach could
     remain reliable as usage grows.
